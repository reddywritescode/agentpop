Pill badge for tags, tones, and counts (use StatusBadge for lifecycle states).

```jsx
<Badge tone="accent">Team</Badge>
<Badge tone="outline" size="sm">v1.4.2</Badge>
<Badge tone="success" icon={<Icon name="circle-check" size={14} />}>Verified</Badge>
```

Tones: neutral, accent, success, warning, danger, info, outline. Sizes sm/md.
